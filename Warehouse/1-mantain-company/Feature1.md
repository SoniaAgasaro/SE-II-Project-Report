# Feature1: Warehouse Maintenance
**Feature ID:** 1
**Branch pattern:** `feature/1-warehouse-maintenance`
**Created:** 2026-09-28
**Input:** Maintaining warehouse information used to identify where inventory is stored

## User Stories

### US-1.1: Maintaining warehouse information
**As an** office employee
**I want to** add, edit, or delete warehouse information
**So that** I keep warehouse current information


## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The system MUST allow an authorized office employee to add warehouse information.
- **FR-002**: The system MUST allow an authorized office employee to edit warehouse information.
- **FR-003**: The system MUST allow an authorized office employee to delete warehouse information.

## Acceptance Criteria

### US-1.1 — Maintain warehouse information

#### Scenario: Warehouse information can be added
* **Given** warehouse information is entered
* **When** the system saves the warehouse information
* **Then** the warehouse information is available for later use
